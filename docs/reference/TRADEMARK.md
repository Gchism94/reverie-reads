software-relevant classes: **IC 9** (downloadable apps), **IC 42** (web/SaaS), **IC 41**
(online content/entertainment).

> **Not legal advice.** This reflects only US registered/pending records that are
> publicly indexed. It misses common-law (unregistered) names in use, foreign marks, and
> very recent filings. Before committing a name commercially, get a trademark attorney's

## Recommendation

- **Gloaming** is the cleanest in-class option and fits the dusk aesthetic.
- **Nocturne** and **Lagniappe** are workable in the software class but are common words
  worth a deeper sweep.
- **Vesper** and **Belladonna** are the most cautionary.
- Keeping **Reverie** means coexisting with an existing same-class (different-field) mark.

Note: "Nocturne" and "Magnolia Dawn" currently serve as the two **theme** names, which is
a low-risk use regardless of the app name decision.
