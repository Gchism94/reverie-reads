# Reverie Reads Discover: 2020+ Curated Title List

This document is a title-candidate list for corpus-gap remediation in Reverie Reads Discover. It is intentionally limited to identifiable titles plus rationale signals; it does **not** attempt ISBN resolution, cover sourcing, or structured bibliographic enrichment. The nine top-level genres follow the app brief: Romance, Fantasy, Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery, Literary, Cozy, Nonfiction, and Young Adult. Bucket labels are **Popular/Bestseller**, **Cult Following**, and **Notable Indie**.

## Notes

- Cozy is treated as a distinct top-level category, even when individual titles also plausibly fit Mystery.
- Some titles are cross-tab candidates by nature, especially cozy mysteries and cozy fantasy; that is not, by itself, proof of a rail-duplication defect in Discover.
- "Notable Indie" is treated here as **self-published plus small-press**, pending scope confirmation.
- "Likely ASIN-only / KU-heavy" means the title surfaces primarily through Amazon/Kindle ecosystems and should be checked before a standard ISBN-resolution attempt.
