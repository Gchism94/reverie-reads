# Claude Design prompt — "Your Reading, Wrapped" (standalone, skin-aware)

Paste into Claude Design. Stands alone. The annual reading recap as shareable DATA-ART — a vertical,
scroll-snapped story of a year of reading, rendered in the reader's active skin.
NAMING: the app is "Reverie" (that's the wordmark on the share card). The default/romance skin is
"Tryst" (gothic New Orleans). Don't confuse the two — Reverie = product, Tryst = the skin it's
rendered in here.

```
Design "YOUR READING, WRAPPED" for Reverie, a genre-neutral personal library app — a year-in-review
the reader scrolls through, then shares. It renders in the reader's ACTIVE skin (a "skin" is the
app's visual identity: palette, type, ambient atmosphere). Render the whole flow in the default skin
"Tryst" (romance / gothic New Orleans — display Fraunces, body Hanken Grotesk; dark Nocturne bg
#0b0612, ink #f6e9f1, primary #e83a78, gold #f0b14e), lean into dark mode for drama, and re-render
ONE panel in an alternate skin (Aphelion — Space Grotesk, deep space, cyan #4fd1e0 on bg #05070d) to
prove it inherits whatever skin is active.

This is DATA-ART, not a dashboard — each panel is a full-bleed "moment": one big idea, huge
expressive type, the skin's ambient signature behind it, numbers that animate in on scroll,
scroll-snap between panels. Use the reader's data shape (invent plausible numbers):

PANELS (in order):
1. Opening — "Your year in books · [year]" + the headline number (books finished); the night-sky
   signature at full strength; the Reverie wordmark small in a corner.
2. Volume — books, pages, and audiobook hours; this year vs last (a tasteful delta).
3. Rhythm — a calendar/heatmap of reading days; longest streak; busiest month.
4. Taste — the genre mix as an expressive visual (note: this is the same signal that feeds the
   adaptive skin) + top tags/tropes (skin-labeled — the Tryst skin says "tropes").
5. Standouts — book of the year, highest-rated, longest, fastest read, most reread.
6. Series — series advanced and finished; gaps closed.
7. Format & ownership — physical / ebook / audio split; owned vs borrowed.
8. Intensity — the intensity (Tryst: "spice") distribution across the year, skin-labeled; show how
   this panel gracefully hides for skins/readers without an intensity signal.
9. People — most-read authors; new-to-you author count.
10. The Share Card — a single composed, on-brand, skin-aware summary image (the one you'd post),
    with the Reverie wordmark and a "Share / Save image" action.

Each panel: AA contrast, visible focus on interactive bits, and a reduced-motion variant (numbers
appear without the count-up; ambient calms). Sentence case, warm but not cloying copy.

DELIVERABLES:
1) The full scroll — all 10 panels — in the Tryst skin (dark; show light for at least the opening +
   share card).
2) The shareable summary card as its own polished artboard (Reverie wordmark).
3) One panel (e.g. Taste or Standouts) re-rendered in the Aphelion skin to prove skin inheritance.
```
