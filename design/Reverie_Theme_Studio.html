The three, named

Each palette is a time of night over the same street. Keep Reverie as the app and let these be its three moods — or borrow one as the name itself.

Nocturne
Deep gaslit midnight — magenta, violet, crimson, midnight blue. (Dark mode)
Magnolia Dawn
Morning light through lace; the same jewels, dressed for day. (Light mode)
Gloaming
The blue hour — dusk-rose fading up into bruised indigo. (In between)

If you ever rename the app

Gothic, warm, unmistakably the Quarter — read this list out loud and see which one you'd want on the home screen.

Vesper
The evening star; the first hush of night.
Gloaming
Twilight itself — literary, cozy, a little witchy.
Vieux Carré
Literally "the Quarter." Old-world and unmistakable.
Lamplight
Gaslit, intimate, the glow you read by.
Velvet Hour
Plush and romantic — the hour you curl up with a book.
Belladonna
Beautiful nightshade. Gothic romance in a single word.
Lagniappe
New Orleans for "a little something extra."
Wisteria
Night-blooming, draped over an iron balcony.
